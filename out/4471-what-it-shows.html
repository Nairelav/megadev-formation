  1. The shared parser reads dates month first: 374 reports. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Day and month swap. Issue #1.
  2. The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY: 33 reports. People at booking-v3 tenants type the month first because the hint tells them to, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The swap looks the same, but the fix is the form hint, not the parser. Issue #2.
  3. Dates move by one day: 5 reports. Nothing swaps. The date lands one day early or late. One report has the same meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. This looks like a time zone bug, and the parser fix will not touch it. Issue #3.

The ticket export has 427 rows. 15 of them repeat a report, so they are counted once.

What the #4471 page gets wrong

  • Its cause table counts the 5 one-day moves as parser reports and only mentions them in a footnote.
  • It marks the hint row “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”, so that bug has no ticket at all.
  • Its “tagged as” column shows intake’s guess, made from the reporter’s browser. For 38 reports that guess disagrees with the cause the desk's own logic gives.
  • Its timeline shows a spike that is not a surge. Nothing was merged for 13 weeks, then 184 reports were merged in one go on 2026-07-02. Reports came in at a steady rate the whole time.

How the problems were told apart

Every count on this page is computed from reports-4471.json by #lib/kb/problems.ts, which reuses the desk’s own causes.ts and locales.ts. A report goes to “one-day” when its text shows the date moving by one day. Otherwise, a report sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant goes to “hint” and the rest go to “parser”.
